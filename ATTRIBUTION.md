# Data Attribution

Portions of the training corpus for Mira are derived from **Dolma**, an open
dataset by the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), used under the
[ODC-By 1.0 license](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

> Soldaini et al., "Dolma: an Open Corpus of Three Trillion Tokens for
> Language Model Pretraining Research", 2024. https://github.com/allenai/dolma

Synthetic dialogue data is generated by `make_voice_dataset.py` in this repo.
Fine-tuned variants of Mira may derive from Apache-2.0 base models
(SmolLM2, Qwen2.5); see each model's card for its license.
